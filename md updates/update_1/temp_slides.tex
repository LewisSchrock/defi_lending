\begin{frame}{[TEMP] Multi-Layer Pipeline Architecture}

\textbf{Each observation passes through six processing layers.}

\vspace{0.3cm}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Configuration} --- 51 CSUs defined across 14 chains; protocol, version, registry address, RPC endpoint per CSU
    \item \textbf{Block mapping} --- Binary search maps calendar dates to block numbers; cached per chain per year
    \item \textbf{TVL extraction} --- 166 on-chain calls per snapshot; reads 27 reserve markets $\times$ 6 fields each
    \item \textbf{Price conversion} --- Chainlink oracle lookups for 22 non-stablecoin assets; results cached daily
    \item \textbf{Liquidation scanning} --- Unified scanner decodes 8 protocol-specific event signatures in one pass; handles Aave, Compound V3, Fluid, SparkLend, Moonwell, Venus, Cap, Gearbox
    \item \textbf{Bronze $\to$ Silver $\to$ Gold} --- Aggregate, merge, balance panel; produce analysis-ready output
\end{enumerate}

\vspace{0.2cm}

\begin{block}{Full project scope}
42 CSUs, 14 chains, 2 years $\rightarrow$ estimated \textbf{$\sim$15 million smart contract calls} for complete data collection.
\end{block}

\end{frame}
