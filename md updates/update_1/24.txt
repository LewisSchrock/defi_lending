Key Bottlenecks
1. Sequential vs Parallel Execution
Problem: Original collector used one RPC key at a time, cycling through when rate-limited. This meant 12 keys sat idle while 1 worked.

Solution Implemented: Created collect_liquidations_parallel.py that runs 13 workers simultaneously, each with its own Alchemy key. Theoretical 13× speedup.

2. Rate Limit Management
Problem: eth_getLogs is expensive (~75 CU per call on Alchemy). At 300 CU/sec limit per key, that's only ~4 getLogs calls/second per key.

Current approach:

0.3 second pace per worker (conservative)
Per-worker rate limiting (independent backoff)
Failed chunks return to queue for other workers
Tradeoff: More aggressive pacing = faster collection but risk of 429s and wasted capacity during backoff periods.

3. Block Range Chunking
Current: 10-block chunks for getLogs calls

Smaller chunks = more calls but less risk of "response too large" errors
Larger chunks = fewer calls but may hit response limits on active blocks
4. Provider-Specific Quirks
Alchemy: Most reliable, consistent CU pricing, good error messages
BlockPi: Per-chain keys (good for isolation), but shared monthly pool
Infura: High-credit cost for eth_getLogs, daily (not monthly) limits
Public RPCs: Unreliable, aggressive rate limiting
Strategic Decisions Needed
1. Parallelism Level
Conservative (current): 13 workers, 0.3s pace = ~43 calls/sec total
Aggressive: 13 workers, 0.1s pace = ~130 calls/sec total
Risk: Burning through monthly quotas faster, more 429 errors
2. Chain Prioritization
Option A: Complete Ethereum first (most CSUs, most data)
Option B: Parallel multi-chain (spread risk, get partial data everywhere)

3. Quota Allocation
Current month quota consumption: Unknown (need to check provider dashboards)
If running low, may need to throttle or wait for reset
4. Data Completeness vs Speed
Current end date: 2025-12-31 (fixed, not extending to present)
Partial data acceptable? Or need 100% coverage?
