    \subsection{7. Key Finding: Opposite Signs by Base Asset
Type}\label{key-finding-opposite-signs-by-base-asset-type}

The central empirical result: the response of collateral volatility to a
utilization shock \textbf{reverses sign} depending on the base asset
type of the Compound V3 isolated market.

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Stablecoin base markets} (USDC/USDT/USDS): Utilization ↑ →
  Volatility \textbf{↓} (negative)
\item
  \textbf{Volatile base markets} (WETH/wstETH/AERO): Utilization ↑ →
  Volatility \textbf{↑} (positive)
\end{itemize}

    \subsection{12. Interpretation Summary}\label{interpretation-summary}

\subsubsection{Key Findings}\label{key-findings}

\textbf{1. Volatility Granger-causes utilization (deleveraging
channel)}\\
Significant in Full Sample (p=0.024), Isolated (p=0.024), Stablecoin
(p=0.006), and Volatile (p=0.006) markets. When collateral volatility
rises, utilization adjusts --- consistent with borrowers deleveraging or
being liquidated during stress.

\textbf{2. Utilization → Volatility depends on market structure}\\
Only significant in stablecoin base markets (p\textless0.001). The
response sign reverses: - \textbf{Stablecoin base (−):} Higher
utilization is associated with subsequent \emph{lower} collateral
volatility. Consistent with procyclical leverage --- borrowing expands
during calm markets. - \textbf{Volatile base (+):} Higher utilization is
associated with subsequent \emph{higher} collateral volatility.
Consistent with reflexive leverage --- ETH borrowing amplifies
collateral price movements.

\textbf{3. Pooled vs Isolated architecture}\\
Pooled protocols (Aave etc.) show a negative U→V response similar to
stablecoin markets, which makes sense --- their aggregate utilization is
dominated by stablecoin borrowing.
